## 🌟 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my **Data Technician Bootcamp**, focusing on using **Microsoft Power BI** to clean, transform, model, analyse and visualise retail and sales data.

Using the **Adventure Works sales dataset**, I developed practical business intelligence skills by working with Power Query, DAX calculations, interactive reports and performance dashboards.

The objective was to transform raw business data into meaningful visual insights, enabling stakeholders to monitor sales performance, identify trends, evaluate profitability and make informed business decisions.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- 🟡 **Microsoft Power BI** – Interactive reports and dashboards
- 🟢 **Power Query Editor** – Data cleaning and transformation
- 🔵 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Calculated columns and measures
- 🟣 **Power BI Data Modelling** – Table relationships and semantic models
- 🟠 **Excel & Structured Datasets** – Data sources
- 📊 **Power BI Visualisations** – Charts, tables, KPI cards and maps

---

## 🚀 Key Skills Demonstrated

### 🧹 1. Data Cleaning & Transformation Using Power Query

Used Power Query to prepare raw datasets for analysis.

- Imported and connected to structured datasets.
- Reviewed and corrected data types.
- Filtered unnecessary records and columns.
- Explored techniques for handling missing values and duplicates.
- Renamed columns and applied transformations.
- Prepared clean, structured tables for reporting.
- Used applied steps to support repeatable data preparation.

### 🧮 2. DAX Calculations & Data Modelling

Developed skills in **Data Analysis Expressions (DAX)** to create calculated columns and measures.

- Created measures to calculate sales, profit and profit margins.
- Analysed sales against business targets.
- Used aggregations to summarise business performance.
- Explored calculated columns for row-level analysis.
- Worked with semantic models and relationships between datasets.

**Key business metrics explored:**

- 💰 Total Sales
- 📈 Total Profit
- 🎯 Sales Targets
- 📉 Sales Variance
- 📊 Profit Margin

### 🎛️ 3. Interactive Dashboards & Reports

Developed interactive Power BI reports enabling users to explore retail performance.

- Created report pages for sales overview, profitability and individual performance.
- Applied slicers and filters to analyse data by region, year and product category.
- Used interactive visualisations to support deeper investigation.
- Designed KPI cards to highlight performance against targets.
- Organised reports with clear layouts and consistent formatting.

### 📊 4. Data Visualisation

Used a variety of visualisations to communicate business insights.

| Visualisation | Business Purpose |
|---|---|
| 📊 Bar Charts | Compare product sales and category performance |
| 📈 Line Charts | Analyse monthly sales trends |
| 🥧 Pie Charts | Illustrate proportions across sales categories |
| 🗺️ Maps | Explore geographical sales distribution |
| 🎯 KPI Cards | Monitor sales, targets and variance |
| 📋 Tables & Matrices | Present detailed sales and profitability results |
| 📉 Combination Charts | Compare sales performance against trends or targets |

### 🛍️ 5. Retail & Sales Performance Analysis

Analysed Adventure Works retail data to understand business performance.

- Compared sales across product groups and categories.
- Evaluated sales and profit margins.
- Analysed actual sales against targets.
- Explored salesperson performance.
- Compared monthly sales results.
- Examined regional variations in sales activity.
- Identified potential areas for operational and commercial improvement.

### 📖 6. Data Storytelling & Business Insights

Focused on presenting analytical findings through clear, engaging visualisations.

- Transformed complex numerical data into accessible business reports.
- Used colours, charts and KPI indicators to highlight performance differences.
- Structured dashboards to guide users from headline performance to detailed analysis.
- Communicated sales trends and variances visually.
- Supported evidence-based decision-making through clear performance reporting.
